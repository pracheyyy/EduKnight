Questions

Everything you're probably wondering

Is EduKnight free to use?

Yes — practice sets, daily quizzes and battles are free. A premium tier unlocks full mock test series and deeper analytics.

How do 1v1 battles work?

Create a room, share the invite code, and once your friend joins you both get the same question set with a live, synced timer.

Which exams are supported?

NEET, JEE (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) and MHT-CET, each with its own subjects, chapters and mock test pattern.

Can I use EduKnight on mobile?

Yes, the entire platform is mobile-first and works smoothly from a 320px screen up to ultra-wide desktops.
